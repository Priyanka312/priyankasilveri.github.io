Hello,

I'm a Master's student at Drexel University, diving deep into the world of Machine Learning. My journey in technology, however, started a long time ago in India. I still remember being a sixth-grader, completely mesmerized by the simple 'Paint' tool on a computer. It felt like magic, and that spark of curiosity about how machines could be 'taught' things never really left me. That curiosity turned into a clear-cut mission when I was ten. I lost my father to cancer, and in the midst of trying to understand such a huge loss, I started reading newspaper articles about researchers using technology to build predictive algorithms for early cancer detection. That was it for me. I knew I wanted to be a part of that but using technology to make a tangible, positive impact on people's lives.
This goal became my driving force. It pushed me through summer coding bootcamps as a kid and then through my entire undergraduate degree in Computer Science. I'll be honest, some of it was tough—I really struggled with algorithm analysis at first. But I was so dedicated to my 'why' that I found a different way to learn. Instead of just focusing on theory, I spent my time practicing on platforms like Kaggle and Hackerrank, which not only helped me master the concepts but also introduced me to new ways of problem-solving. This hands-on approach paid off, landing me an internship and eventually a fantastic full-time job at Teradata, where I got to work on real-world data problems.
But that dream of building AI for healthcare, like creating a system that could give doctors a 48-hour head start on a life-threatening illness, was still my ultimate goal. I knew that to build something that complex, I needed to deepen my expertise, which is exactly why I'm now at Drexel.
While tech and AI are my professional focus, they aren't my whole life. I am also a trained Bharatnatyam dancer. For me, dance is a beautiful form of self-expression and discipline; I love performing at temples and sharing that part of my culture. It’s the perfect creative balance to my analytical work. Ultimately, I'm just someone who is deeply passionate about learning, driven to solve meaningful problems, and always looking for a way to bring a little more joy to the world, whether it's through code or dance.
